title: Record a call
description: Record outbound, inbound, or live calls, pause and stop recordings, and download audio in WAV or MP3.
---

<Note>
  Recorded time is billed per recorded second at your account's recording rate. Recording rates are not listed in `GET /prices` yet — contact Sendblue for your rate, or inspect the settled `price` on each completed recording and in `GET /admin/billing/transactions`.

  Notifying call participants that a call is being recorded, where required by law, is your responsibility.
</Note>

## Start a recording

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On an outbound call">
    Pass `"record": true` to `POST /calls` to record from answer to hangup:

    ```bash
    curl https://omni-api.sendblue.com/calls \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDBLUE_KEY_ID.$SENDBLUE_KEY_SECRET" \
      -H "Idempotency-Key: 3d0a5b1c-call-0002" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "to": "+14155550101",
        "from": "+14155550100",
        "twiml": "<Response><Say>This call is recorded.</Say><Pause length=\"30\"/></Response>",
        "record": true,
        "recording_channels": "dual"
      }'
    ```

    - `recording_channels`: `mono` or `dual`.
    - `recording_track`: `inbound`, `outbound`, or `both`.
    - `recording_status_callback`: receives `in_progress`, `completed`, and `absent` updates in Twilio's callback format (`X-Twilio-Signature`).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="On a call in progress">
    Start a recording on a live call, inbound or outbound, with `POST /calls/{call_id}/recordings`. Every body member is optional; with no body, both parties are recorded on one channel:

    ```bash
    curl https://omni-api.sendblue.com/calls/CA0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef/recordings \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDBLUE_KEY_ID.$SENDBLUE_KEY_SECRET" \
      -H "Idempotency-Key: 3d0a5b1c-recording-0001" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "channels": "dual",
        "status_callback": "https://example.com/recordings",
        "status_callback_events": ["in_progress", "completed"]
      }'
    ```

    The `201` response is the recording with `"status": "processing"`; it becomes `in_progress` once the carrier has acknowledged the start.

    - `channels`: `mono` (default) or `dual`. `dual` needs `track` to be `both`.
    - `track`: `inbound`, `outbound`, or `both` (default).
    - `trim`: `do_not_trim` (default) or `trim_silence`.
    - `status_callback`: receives the events named in `status_callback_events` (`completed` alone when absent) in Twilio's callback format (`X-Twilio-Signature`).

    The request answers `409 conflict` when the call is not in progress or already has a recording running, and `402 insufficient_funds` when your balance does not cover the longest recording the call can still make. Repeating it with the same `Idempotency-Key` returns the recording the first request started.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="With TwiML <Record>">
    To record an incoming call — or only part of a call — use the `<Record>` verb in your TwiML document:

    ```xml
    <?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
    <Response>
      <Say>Please leave a message after the tone.</Say>
      <Record maxLength="60"/>
    </Response>
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Pause, resume, or stop a recording

Change a recording that is running with `PATCH /recordings/{recording_id}`:

```bash
curl -X PATCH https://omni-api.sendblue.com/recordings/RE0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDBLUE_KEY_ID.$SENDBLUE_KEY_SECRET" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"status": "paused"}'
```

- `{"status": "paused"}` pauses capture.
- `{"status": "in_progress"}` resumes it.
- `{"status": "stopped"}` ends the recording; it moves to `processing` and then `completed`.

The change is queued for the carrier: the `200` response is the recording as it is now, and its `status` follows once the carrier has acknowledged. Asking for the state a recording is already in succeeds and changes nothing. A recording that is not in a state the change applies to answers `409 conflict`.

## Recording callbacks

Recording status callbacks, and the `action` requests of `<Record>` and of a recorded `<Dial>`, carry a `RecordingUrl`. It is the recording's audio route on this API, `https://omni-api.sendblue.com/recordings/{recording_id}/audio`, and needs the same bearer credentials as the download below. It answers with WAV audio; add `?format=mp3` for MP3. Appending `.mp3` or `.wav` to the link does not select a format.

## Retrieve and manage recordings

<Steps>
  <Step title="List a call's recordings">
    ```bash
    curl https://omni-api.sendblue.com/calls/CA0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef/recordings \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDBLUE_KEY_ID.$SENDBLUE_KEY_SECRET"
    ```

    Each recording includes `id`, `status`, `channels` (`1` or `2`), `duration_seconds` (whole seconds), and `price`. `status` is one of `in_progress`, `paused`, `processing`, `completed`, or `absent` (a recording with no audio or that failed).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the audio">
    Wait until `status` is `completed`, then fetch the audio stream:

    ```bash
    curl "https://omni-api.sendblue.com/recordings/RE0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef/audio?format=mp3" \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDBLUE_KEY_ID.$SENDBLUE_KEY_SECRET" \
      -o recording.mp3
    ```

    `format` accepts `wav` (default) or `mp3`. Audio is served only to your account's bearer credentials — no public or unauthenticated link exists.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Delete a recording">
    ```bash
    curl -X DELETE https://omni-api.sendblue.com/recordings/RE0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $SENDBLUE_KEY_ID.$SENDBLUE_KEY_SECRET"
    ```

    A `204` response confirms the recording and its audio have been deleted.
  </Step>
</Steps>
