Send and receive messages over Sendblue-managed US iMessage lines through the same messaging and webhook surface you use for the rest of the Omni API. Instead of registering a 10DLC campaign, you submit a number order for iMessage lines; once Sendblue sets them up, they appear directly on your account ready to send from `POST /messages`.

## How P2P iMessage works on Omni

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your iMessage number price">
    Call `GET /prices` and check for `number.imessage.request`. If it is absent, contact Sendblue to have a rate assigned to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request iMessage lines">
    Submit `POST /numbers/orders` with `"channel": "imessage"` and your desired `quantity` (`1`–`100`), plus optional `area_code` and `note` preferences. Recording and fulfilling an order do not debit your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and receive">
    Once the order status reaches `fulfilled`, your lines appear in `GET /numbers` with `source: "requested"` and `"imessage"` in `channels`. Pass an assigned number as `from` in `POST /messages` and subscribe a [webhook](/webhooks) to `message.sent`, `message.delivered`, `message.failed`, and `message.received`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## At a glance

| Capability | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Provisioning** | Requested via `POST /numbers/orders` (`channel: "imessage"`, quantity `1`–`100`). Cancel pending orders with `DELETE /numbers/orders/{order_id}`. |
| **Line management** | Managed by Sendblue — fulfilled iMessage lines cannot be configured, assigned to a 10DLC campaign, or released through the API. |
| **Sending** | Pass an assigned iMessage number as `from` in `POST /messages` with an `Idempotency-Key`. |
| **Attachments** | Received attachments (photos including HEIC, video, audio, PDF, voice notes, contact cards; 4 MB a file, 16 MB a message) arrive in `media_urls` and are downloaded with your bearer credentials. Sending attachments from an iMessage line is not available today: a send with media is refused. See [Attachments you receive](/imessage-messaging#attachments-you-receive). |
| **Delivery evidence** | `Message` records `delivery_evidence` (`verified`, `reported`, `reported_unconfirmed`, or `unknown`), `billing_status` (`held`, `charged`, `uncharged`, or `review`), and `channel` (`imessage` or `sms`) on lines that can fall back to device SMS. |
| **Permissions** | `number_orders:create`, `number_orders:read`, `number_orders:cancel`, `messages:send`, and `messages:read`. |
